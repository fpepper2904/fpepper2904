## Current research

**Single-cell characterization of neutrophil subpopulations** · KU Leuven, 2026

Multimodal single-cell work on immune cell heterogeneity across tissues in mouse models, combining transcriptomic and surface-protein measurements.

- Built and validated a cell-subtype annotation for a multi-tissue neutrophil atlas, from QC and integration through clustering and marker-based labelling
- Performed **reference label transfer** against a published neutrophil atlas to benchmark an in-house annotation against community nomenclature, and quantified concordance subtype by subtype
- Ran paired **RNA and ADT (CITE-seq)** analyses to identify surface markers separating subtypes — translating computational findings into panels that can be validated by flow cytometry
- Designed the statistical approach around the experimental unit: per-animal directional summaries rather than cell-level tests, to avoid pseudoreplication in a small-cohort design

*Manuscript in preparation — analysis code and results are held privately pending publication.*
